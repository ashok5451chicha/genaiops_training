## Part B – how it was completed here

* The lab Q1 prompt was run as a fresh conversation (identical text, no system prompt, no history) through the
  Anthropic API on Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5, the models the Claude.ai app offers. Results are in
  `outputs/homework_comparison.md` and `outputs/day2_runs/partB_claude_api_Q1.json`.
* **ChatGPT and Gemini were not run**: there is no access to them from this machine. Their columns are marked "not
  run" and nothing was guessed.
* Privacy-setting locations come from the vendors' help pages, not from a logged-in account.
* Real embeddings (needs the Microsoft Visual C++ runtime) were skipped by decision; the toy embedder was used.
